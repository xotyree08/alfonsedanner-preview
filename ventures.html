Venture 01

AD3 Media Group

The RGD Method · Resilience. Growth. Discipline.

AD3 Media Group is the media arm behind the message — the home for Alfonse's publishing, short-form video, and the Did I Win? series. Its job is simple: take a framework that was forged in the hardest room imaginable and put it in front of the people who need it, in the format they actually watch.

  • PublishingBooks and written work built around the RGD Blueprint.
  • Short-Form VideoThe Did I Win? clip series across Instagram, TikTok, and YouTube.
  • Speaking & MediaPodcasts, panels, and stages where the message lands hardest.

Venture 02

Danner Empowerment Foundation

Building Minds. Changing Lives.

The Danner Empowerment Foundation is the non-profit side of the mission: youth initiatives and support for people rebuilding after incarceration. The foundation exists to hand people the mental tools to stand firm, act with integrity, and build a future no sentence can take away.

  • Youth InitiativesMindset and accountability work with young people before the doors ever close.
  • Re-Entry SupportPractical guidance for people walking back into a world that moved on without them.
  • Community WorkshopsRGD taught in the rooms where it matters — schools, programs, and centers.
