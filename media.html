Short bio

Alfonse “Pop” Danner is an author, speaker, and entrepreneur, and the creator of the RGD Blueprint — Resilience, Growth Mindset, and Discipline. Sentenced to life in prison, he served nearly 27 years, taught himself the law, won his own freedom, and rebuilt his life in three years. He is the author of Did I Win? Beyond the Verdict and the founder of AD3 Media Group and the Danner Empowerment Foundation.

Long bio

Alfonse “Pop” Danner stood before a judge who told him he would never walk free again. He served nearly 27 years, losing his mother, his father, and his sister while inside. In that crucible he built the framework that would get him out: the RGD Blueprint — Resilience, Growth Mindset, and Discipline. He taught himself the law, fought his own case through years of denials, and walked out. In the three years since, he has become a published author, founded AD3 Media Group, and started the Danner Empowerment Foundation. A military veteran, he now takes the same framework into schools, re-entry programs, and organizations — through his book Did I Win? Beyond the Verdict, a licensed video curriculum, and live keynotes and workshops.
